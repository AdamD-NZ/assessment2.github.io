Privacy Policy

Snazzy Paws Grooming respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This privacy policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when using the Snazzy Paws Grooming website.

Information We Collect

Snazzy Paws Grooming may collect the following information through the website:

  • Customer names and contact details submitted through booking or enquiry forms
  • Information relating to grooming appointments and requested services
  • Website usage information collected through analytics tools and cookies
  • Communication records relating to customer enquiries or bookings

Why Information Is Collected

The information collected is used to:

  • Respond to customer enquiries and booking requests
  • Manage grooming appointments and customer communication
  • Improve website functionality and user experience
  • Analyse website traffic and visitor behaviour using analytics tools

Website Analytics and Cookies

This website uses Google Analytics to collect anonymous information about how visitors use the website. This may include pages visited, time spent on the website, device type and general location information.

Cookies may be stored on the user’s device to support analytics and improve website performance. Users may disable cookies through their web browser settings; however, some website functionality may be affected.

How Information Is Protected

Snazzy Paws Grooming takes reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Security measures include password-protected administration areas, secure hosting services and HTTPS encryption where available.

Sharing Information

Personal information will not be sold or shared with third parties unless:

  • Required by law
  • Necessary to provide website or hosting services
  • Authorised by the customer

Providing Information

Providing personal information is optional. However, if required information is not provided, Snazzy Paws Grooming may be unable to process booking requests or respond to customer enquiries.

Accessing and Correcting Information

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, customers have the right to request access to personal information held about them and request corrections if the information is inaccurate.
